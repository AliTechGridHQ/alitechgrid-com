AliTechGrid / FacultyOS
FacultyOS

Make evidence-based, personalized teaching practical—not another reporting burden.

FacultyOS is AliTechGrid’s flagship faculty-support concept: a three-path system for faculty, students and management, backed by sovereign/private AI options and auditable evidence.

F

Faculty Path

Course outline → schedule → notes → slides → activities → labs → assessments → rubrics → feedback → gap-fillers → evidence and reporting.

S

Student Path

Clear learning-progress evidence, diagnosed gaps, targeted mitigation activities, practice, reassessment and visible improvement over time.

M

Management Path

Aggregated and individual performance evidence, intervention effectiveness, course-level trends, governance and quality-assurance reporting.

Faculty problem

UDL and personalization fail when the workload is unrealistic.

FacultyOS is designed around a simple operational truth: instructors often know what good inclusive practice looks like, but they do not have enough time to create multiple learning pathways, activities, assessments and interventions for every learner.

AI drafts; faculty approve and remain academically accountable.
Course artifacts stay aligned to learning outcomes and approved source material.
UDL, formative assessment, feedforward, mastery, retrieval, metacognition and other models are selected when they fit the learning problem.
Student interventions produce traceable before/after evidence.
Management views emphasize outcomes and risk—not surveillance for its own sake.
Signature product

FacultyOS Decision Loop
