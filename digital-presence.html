Responsible AI by design

AI features should match the real business need

Controlled assistant: provides approved responses, navigation and lead routing without sending each question to a language model.

Generative-AI assistant: uses a secured backend and approved knowledge sources to handle broader questions. Model choice, retention, privacy, cost and human oversight must be confirmed.

Workflow automation: connects specific actions—such as enquiry classification, booking, notification or document preparation—without implying that every process needs AI.

Architecture and governance considerations

  • Client ownership of domain, email, hosting, phone numbers and customer data
  • Private handling of passwords, API keys and administrative credentials
  • Approved content and clear limits for AI-generated responses
  • Human review for quotations, contracts, regulated decisions and sensitive matters
  • Privacy, retention, security and data-residency requirements reviewed during scoping
  • Established payment processors used instead of collecting card details in ordinary forms
