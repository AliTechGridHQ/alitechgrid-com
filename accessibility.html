Accessibility

Inclusive access is part of the product design.

AliTechGrid aims for keyboard access, readable contrast, responsive layouts, semantic structure and reduced-motion compatibility across the public website.

Keyboard & focus

Primary interactions are designed for keyboard access with visible focus states and a skip link.

Responsive content

Layouts adapt to desktop, tablet and mobile without requiring horizontal scrolling for core content.

Plain language

Public diagnostic questions emphasize organizational outcomes rather than requiring deep AI technical knowledge.

If you encounter an accessibility barrier, contact contact@alitechgrid.com and describe the page and issue.
