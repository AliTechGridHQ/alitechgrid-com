ADAPT-UDL platform concept

Sovereign AI for the recurring academic course lifecycle

A developing faculty- and institution-facing platform concept intended to support course design, UDL-informed adaptation, assessment, evaluation, reporting and governance without making students direct platform users.

The institutional problem

Conventional LMS platforms are strong at content delivery and administration, but institutions increasingly need a controlled intelligence layer for evidence-aware course production, faculty workflow, assessment quality, adaptive support and management visibility.

ADAPT-UDL is being shaped as a complementary institutional layer rather than a replacement for established LMS platforms.

Design priorities

  • Instructor review and approval remain central
  • Approved evidence and course boundaries guide generation
  • Students receive outputs through existing institutional channels
  • Local or sovereign deployment options support data control
  • Role-based dashboards support academic and operational oversight
Capability model

From course evidence to institutional insight

01

Evidence ingestion

Course outlines, outcomes, policies, approved learning resources and institutional records.

02

Course production

Weekly plans, lessons, labs, quizzes, exams, rubrics, slide content and structured exports.

03

UDL-informed adaptation

Diagnostics, grouping, movement history, feedforward support and flexible instructional pathways.

04

Assessment workflow

Instructor-controlled assessment generation, evaluation support, audit information and approval steps.

05

Role-based dashboards

Views for instructors, academic leadership, administration and IT.

06

Sovereign operation

Local models, controlled repositories, deletion controls and data-residency-aware deployment.

Development status: ADAPT-UDL is an evolving platform concept and prototype program. Features, integrations, validation and commercial availability depend on institutional requirements, testing and approved implementation scope.
